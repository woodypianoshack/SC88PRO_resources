Holding and combining buttons

A held key is a held button, so the manual’s combinations work as written: hold several keys at once, or hold a key while clicking a panel button.

Manual notationOn the keyboardExample
A & B (press together)Press both keys togetherPart monitor: W & E
A + B (hold, then press)Hold the first key, tap the secondDelay send: hold 2, tap I / O
▲ ▼ in menus1 / 2Step through the part parameter menu
◀ held, then ▶Hold the left key, tap the rightChanges a value faster, as on the hardware

Power-on combinations

Some functions are read only at a cold start. Held keys are kept across the power switch, and automatic Factory Reset and Fast Boot are skipped for that boot.

General method

  1. Press Q to switch the power off.
  2. Hold the keys for the combination.
  3. Press Q to switch it on, and keep holding until the display responds.

Startup test mode

  1. Press Q to switch the power off.
  2. Hold I + O (both KEY SHIFT buttons).
  3. Press Q to switch it on.
  4. At the diagnostic prompt, press Tab (PREVIEW).
  5. Press Q to switch the power off when finished.
Restart Device and switching models release all held buttons.
